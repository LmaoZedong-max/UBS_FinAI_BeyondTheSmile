**Professional Risk Alert: USD/CNH**
**Date:** 2025-10-28

**1. Executive Summary**
USD/CNH is under a **negative sentiment regime** (confidence: 94.9%) with a sentiment score of -0.91. The aggregate macro contribution to predicted log realized volatility is **+0.039**, driven primarily by Brent crude oil returns and a rise in China’s 2-year yield. The offshore yuan strengthened to a one-month high of 7.1015, supported by a stronger-than-expected PBZ midpoint fix (7.0881 vs. estimate 7.1146) and trade-deal optimism.

**2. Cross-Asset & Volatility Narrative**
The top macro drivers of predicted volatility are:
- **RET_DRV_BRENT** (+0.026): Rising oil prices are contributing to CNH volatility.
- **CHG_DRV_CN_2Y_YLD_bps** (+0.021): A rise in China’s 2-year yield is a key volatility contributor.
- **RET_DRV_CSI_300** (-0.020): A decline in Chinese equities is dampening volatility.
- **RET_CNY_SPOT** (+0.014): Spot CNH movement is a direct volatility source.
- **RET_DRV_DXY_US_DOLLAR_INDEX** (-0.011): A weaker DXY is reducing USD/CNH volatility.

The dollar index fell 0.11% to 98.84. The PBZ set the yuan midpoint at 7.0881, the strongest since Oct 15, 2024, and above the Reuters estimate of 7.1146. This, combined with Trump-Xi trade deal optimism, boosted CNH. The CNH ATM volatility factor (PC1) is the relevant IV factor.

**3. Positioning Considerations (Illustrative)**
- **Sentiment Signal:** Negative on USD/CNH (sent_signal: 1), indicating bearish USD bias.
- **Key Event Risk:** This week’s central bank meetings (Fed, BoC, ECB, BoJ) and the Trump-Xi meeting in South Korea are critical. A Fed rate cut is priced in, but focus is on QT guidance.
- **Volatility Outlook:** Elevated macro contributions (+0.039) suggest heightened near-term volatility. The PBZ fix and trade headlines are the primary catalysts.
- **Risk:** A breakdown in trade talks or a hawkish Fed surprise could reverse CNH gains. The U.S. government shutdown (day 27) adds macro uncertainty.
